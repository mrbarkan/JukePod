A quitanda is the small neighborhood shop where you buy fruit. You walk in, look over what's fresh, and pick what you like. That's the idea here, for the iPod: you don't have to run what Apple shipped, and there has never been more to choose from.

## What's on the shelves

**Firmware.** Nine projects so far, from [Rockbox](/firmware/rockbox/), which most of the others grow from, to [Rockpod](/firmware/rockpod/) with its digital audio out and [PodBox](/firmware/podbox/) with album art beside every list. Each page says what the firmware adds, which iPods it runs on, how to install it and where to download it.

**Library apps.** The software that fills the iPod with music: [iOpenPod](/apps/iopenpod/), [Stonepod](/apps/stonepod/), [CrateDigger](/apps/cratedigger/), Apple's own Finder and iTunes, and more for every platform.

**Hardware mods.** [Flash storage, bigger batteries, Bluetooth and USB-C](/mods/), each with a note on how it gets along with custom firmware.

**Still on the tree.** Projects that can't be installed yet, such as Helikon OS, a bootloader and firmware being written from scratch for the iPod Classic.

## How to shop

Choose your iPod in the **My iPod** menu and the whole shop narrows to what fits it. Every listing has its own page and a share link, so you can send a friend straight to the firmware you mean.

The status words follow the fruit: **Ripe** is stable, **Ripening** is beta, **Unripe** is experimental and **Preserved** is archived. The plain word always sits next to it.

## What arrives next

- Sign-in, so you can share what you've made.
- A themes gallery, where people post their themes with screenshots.
- More guides, starting with first installs and storage swaps.

If something is missing, [tell us](https://github.com/mrbarkan/JukePod/issues/new). New listings are welcome, and so are corrections.
