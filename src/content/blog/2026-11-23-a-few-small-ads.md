Quitanda is a one-person side project, not a business. To help cover the domain and running costs, the site will show a few small ads from Google.

## What changes

- A few ads appear on the pages, served by Google AdSense.
- Google may use cookies to choose them. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, you'll be asked first, and you can say no.
- Anywhere in the world, you can turn off personalized ads in [Google's My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/), and ad blockers work fine.

## What doesn't change

- Everything stays free, and you can still browse and download without an account.
- Quitanda still runs no analytics and sets no cookies of its own.
- Your account data (name, email, profile, themes) is never sold or given to advertisers.

The [privacy policy](/privacy/#ads) has the details. If something about the ads bothers you, [tell us](/about/#add-or-correct-a-listing).
