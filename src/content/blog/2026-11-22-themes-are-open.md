Quitanda has a new shelf: [themes](/themes/). If you've made a look for Rockbox, Rockpod, PodBox or CrateDigger, you can now share it here, and anyone can download it.

## Sharing one

1. Sign in with the button at the top of the page.
2. Open [Share a theme](/themes/submit/) and choose what the theme is for.
3. Add the theme file and a few screenshots. iPod screen captures stay pixel-sharp.

For CrateDigger, zip the whole `.cdtheme` folder. The page reads the theme's name from it and draws a small preview in the theme's own colors, so it has a picture even before you add screenshots.

## What gets checked

The page looks inside the file before anything is uploaded. It turns away programs, Rockbox plugins and firmware images, and asks for a license file beside any font you bundle. Then a person looks at every theme before it appears in the gallery.

Two rules to save you a rejection: only share what you made or have the right to share, and leave out Apple's fonts, icons and artwork.

Say which version your theme was made for. Themes often break between firmware versions, and that one line saves people a lot of guessing.
